# Plannr logo concepts

Twenty deliberately different visual directions for the Plannr mobile application.
These are concept masters for direction selection, not production app icons.

## Selected direction

Concept 18, the reserve shield, is the selected direction. The extracted logo
mark is available as `18-reserve-shield-transparent.png`; the original opaque
concept remains available as `18-reserve-shield.png`.

All files are 1254 x 1254 PNG images. Concepts 1-10 have transparent backgrounds;
concepts 11-20 have opaque, full-bleed backgrounds. The selected direction should
be redrawn/refined, tested at small sizes, checked against Android and iOS safe
zones, and screened for trademark conflicts before release.

## Prompt set

Every concept used the `logo-brand` use case and was requested as a square
mobile app icon for Plannr, a personal-finance planning app. Shared constraints
were: an original centered mark, a strong silhouette, generous safe margin,
clarity at small sizes, and no text, currency symbols, coins, app-store mockup,
trademarks, or watermark.

The concept-specific prompts were:

1. Minimal emblem where a calm rising horizon and forward path form a subtle P;
   flat geometric fintech treatment in midnight navy, luminous teal, and warm
   sunrise coral.
2. Three interlocking compartments, with one opening upward around a protected
   saving goal; modular Swiss-inspired construction in cobalt, mint, off-white,
   and electric lime.
3. A continuous-line lowercase-P monogram whose stem becomes a route and whose
   bowl becomes a completed planning loop; warm cream, near-black, and a single
   vermilion endpoint.
4. Three orbital rings around one calm goal, with a deliberate break creating
   forward motion; kinetic circular geometry in charcoal, cyan, ultraviolet,
   and white.
5. Three broad forecast layers stepping upward while their negative space forms
   a sheltered arch; softly dimensional paper-cut treatment in forest, jade,
   sage, and sand.
6. One folded origami form that reads as both compass and open map; energetic
   diagonal geometry in tangerine, aubergine, blush, and cream.
7. A seedling growing from a bowl-shaped pocket, with two leaves suggesting
   balanced inflow and outflow; friendly organic curves in butter yellow, moss,
   leaf green, and terracotta.
8. Four rounded bands woven into one stable knot; bold interconnected topology
   in royal blue, turquoise, magenta, and golden yellow.
9. A stable vertical beacon with broad light beams that also read as an upward
   financial envelope; neo-brutalist geometry in ultramarine, signal yellow,
   white, and black.
10. A rounded abstract Plan Pebble with one protected cut-out pocket and a small
    floating goal dot; tactile clay-like rendering in lavender, indigo, mint,
    and peach.

The money-management-focused second set used opaque, full-bleed backgrounds and
the following prompts:

11. One circular value disc divided into spending, saving, and reserve segments,
    with negative space subtly suggesting a P; midnight navy, teal, sky blue,
    and amber.
12. One value token distributed by a simple Y-shaped junction into three large
    financial pockets; ivory, forest green, cobalt, green, terracotta, and gold.
13. Three financial sliders contained by a wallet silhouette, with their
    controls forming a rising diagonal; electric cobalt, yellow, white, and
    near-black.
14. An open two-column ledger with a check-shaped paper fold, representing
    tracking and reconciliation; plum, blush, coral, and mint.
15. A secure vault door merged with a monthly planning dial around a central
    reserve; emerald, pale gold, and bright mint.
16. One incoming cash-flow channel routed through a central control into current
    spending and future reserves; graphite, cyan, white, and green.
17. A contemporary three-rail abacus whose overall silhouette suggests a
    lowercase P; tactile indigo, coral, gold, and mint forms on pale aqua.
18. An upward-opening shield embracing three stored value tokens, representing
    an accessible emergency fund; orange, burgundy, cream, and pale yellow.
19. Two pocket shapes balancing incoming and outgoing value tokens on one curved
    beam; sage, black, cream, coral, and blue.
20. Three recurring-payment tokens circulating on one loop and passing through
    a visible review gate; midnight violet, aqua, coral, and lime.
